The founder

RBP was founded by Harry Rao following a career spanning international publishing, international property, luxury, media, sales and business development.

His experience includes senior commercial work with International Property Media within the international property sector, alongside more than a decade of involvement with FOUR International, an award-winning food and lifestyle publication working across luxury hospitality, gastronomy, Michelin-starred chefs and premium brands.

Working internationally has given Harry the opportunity to work with business owners, entrepreneurs, senior executives and decision-makers across property, luxury, hospitality and a wide range of commercial sectors.

Over time, he recognised that his strongest skill was not limited to one industry. It was understanding businesses, developing relationships, spotting commercial opportunities and finding practical ways to move them forward. RBP Consultancy was created to take that experience beyond publishing – working with good people, exploring interesting opportunities, helping businesses progress and developing projects worth being part of.
