About RBP

A flexible international platform for growth.

RBP Consultancy was established to create a flexible international platform for business growth, commercial projects and new opportunities.

Our story

Somebody prepared to help get things moving.

We believe businesses do not always need another report or presentation. Sometimes they need somebody prepared to understand the problem, explore the opportunity and help get things moving.

Our approach combines commercial thinking, international experience and relationships developed across multiple industries and markets.

The Founder

Harry Rao

RBP Consultancy was founded by Harry Rao, drawing on more than a decade of international experience across business development, property, publishing, partnerships and luxury markets.

Through his work with International Property Media and FOUR International, Harry has built relationships across global markets, connecting with high-net-worth individuals, entrepreneurs, business owners and senior decision-makers throughout the UK, Europe, the Middle East, the Americas and beyond.

His international involvement also includes membership of the Dubai–Canada Chamber of Commerce and connections across the GCC, extending a network developed through years of working across different industries, cultures and commercial environments.

Throughout his career, Harry recognised that the right conversation, introduction or relationship can often create opportunities that conventional approaches miss. Understanding who to speak to, what each side is looking for and how to bring the right people together became a natural part of his work. That thinking led to RBP Consultancy.

Today, RBP combines Harry’s experience and international relationships with a wider network of specialists to help businesses enter new markets, develop partnerships, identify opportunities and make valuable connections.

Our approach is straightforward: understand the opportunity, connect the right people and turn conversations into commercial progress.
